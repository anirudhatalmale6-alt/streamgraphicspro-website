Privacy Policy

StreamGraphics Pro, by Manhattan Beach Studios LLC ("MBS", "we", "us").

The short version: StreamGraphics Pro runs on your own computer. Your graphics, designs, media and show content stay on your machine — we never see them. The only personal information we handle is what you give us to buy and support a license (mainly your name and email). We don't sell your data.

1. Who we are

Manhattan Beach Studios LLC, 1600 Rosecrans Ave., Bldg 7/101, Manhattan Beach, CA 90266, United States. Questions about privacy? Reach us through our Contact page.

2. Information we collect

  • Purchase & account information — your name, email address, and license/order details when you buy StreamGraphics Pro or contact us for support.
  • Support communications — the content of messages you send us, so we can help you.
  • Website information — basic, standard server logs (such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited) that almost every website records, and any analytics we may add to understand general usage. The site does not require you to log in.

The software itself does not upload your designs, media, or show content. License activation works offline. The app may make a simple, anonymous check to our website to see whether a newer version is available; this can be turned off and carries no personal information.

3. How we use your information

  • To deliver your license key and let you activate the software.
  • To provide support, updates and important notices about the product.
  • To process payments (through our payment provider — see below).
  • To protect against fraud, misuse and license sharing, and to meet legal obligations.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by our payment provider, Square (Block, Inc.). When you buy a license you are also subject to the payment provider's terms and privacy policy, shown during checkout. We do not receive or store your full card details — the payment provider handles them.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business — for example, our payment provider and the email service we use to send license keys and support replies — and only as needed to provide the service. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

6. Data retention

We keep purchase and license records for as long as needed to support your license, honor upgrades, meet legal and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. You can ask us to delete information we are not legally required to keep.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you non-essential messages. Contact us and we'll take care of it.

EEA/UK (GDPR): if you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Our legal bases for processing are performance of our contract with you (providing the license and support), our legitimate interests (running and securing the business), and compliance with legal obligations. Where a Data Processing Agreement applies, the terms referenced in the License Agreement govern that processing.

8. Children

StreamGraphics Pro is a professional production tool intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe and to limit what we collect in the first place.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll post the new version here. Your continued use of the software or website after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or request, please use our Contact page or write to us at the address above.

This Privacy Policy works alongside the License Agreement and the Terms.
