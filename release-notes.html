What's new

StreamGraphics Pro tells you when there's a newer version. Download it and install over the top — your presets, templates, saved shows and scripts are left exactly as they are.

1.0.40

  • Section headings you can actually find. The headings on every control panel now sit on their own bar instead of blending in with the labels beneath them, so a long panel is easier to scan mid-show.
  • The linear gradient is easier to find. On a Box or BG layer the Fill style menu now says "Gradient (linear)" and "Gradient (radial)" rather than leaving you to guess which was which. Same two gradients as before, clearer names.
  • The video layer now says it wants an .mp4 or .webm file, and tells you so if you paste a YouTube or Vimeo page address instead of a video file.
  • On a Mac, the update notice now offers the download that matches your machine — an Intel Mac is no longer pointed at the Apple Silicon version.

1.0.39

Mac
  • StreamGraphics Pro runs on a Mac. Separate downloads for Apple Silicon (M1 and later) and Intel Macs, alongside the Windows version. Same app, same panels, same output links.
  • On a Mac your work is kept in your own Library folder rather than inside the app, so replacing the app with a newer one never touches it.
  • The font menu now offers typefaces that exist on both Windows and Mac, so a graphic you design on one looks the same on the other. Worth knowing if you share or sell designs.

1.0.38

  • Choose your output. One box on every control panel. It sets how the graphic leaves the app — transparent for OBS and vMix, a chroma color, a luma key, or key and fill for a hardware switcher — and hands you the exact address to paste in.
  • Recolor a picture layer without leaving the app: tint it to a color you pick, or shift its hue. Either can be driven from a spreadsheet column, so one design can carry a different team's color on every row.
  • New Head to Head template — two competitors, photos, and a row of stats each.

1.0.37

  • Send an output to a monitor. Any panel can put its output full screen on a screen you choose, and take it off again from the same place. It remembers the monitor by name, so unplugging a screen between shows will not put the next one on the wrong one.

1.0.36

  • Football and basketball scoreboards. A real game clock, the shot clock or play clock beside it, period, fouls, timeouts and possession — driven from a control panel and matching on every screen showing it.

1.0.35

  • Designs say what they need. A graphic can declare the things that get filled in — a name, a title, a photo, a color — so putting one on air is a short form to fill, instead of hunting through layers for the right text box.

1.0.34

  • Key and fill, for hardware switchers. Two outputs instead of one: your graphic on black, and a matching white silhouette. Wire them to the switcher's fill and key inputs and it is told what is transparent rather than guessing from a color. Soft edges, drop shadows and see-through panels survive intact.

1.0.33

  • Framing guides on the design canvas — title safe, action safe, center lines, thirds, and vertical crop markings for anything headed to a phone screen. On while you design, never on the output.

Running an older version? Open StreamGraphics Pro and it will tell you when an update is ready, or grab the latest download. Something not working the way you expect? Tell me — I read every message.
