Frequently asked questions

What is StreamGraphics Pro?

A tool for creating and running live broadcast graphics — lower thirds, scoreboards, timers, slides and score bugs — that key straight into OBS, vMix, or any software with a browser source. You design in your browser and drive it live from any device on your network.

Do I need OBS or vMix?

You need something that can show a web page as a video source — OBS (Browser Source), vMix (Web Browser input), or similar. You can also feed a chroma-colour version to a hardware switcher, or use full-screen graphics directly.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The app runs entirely on your own computer over your local network — perfect for venues with spotty internet. (The optional Remote Scoreboard add-on is the one feature that uses the internet.)

Is there a subscription?

No monthly fee. It's free to use with a small watermark, and a one-time upgrade removes the watermark and unlocks the pro features. Add-ons are optional.

How does the watermark work?

The free version shows a small "StreamGraphics Pro" mark in the corner of the output. Enter a license key and it disappears — the control panels are never watermarked.

Can multiple people run it at once?

Yes. Anyone on the same network can open a control panel — e.g. a scorer on a tablet and a director on a laptop — and everything stays in sync in real time.

Can someone run a scoreboard remotely?

Same-network is built in. For an operator in another location, the optional Remote Scoreboard add-on connects them over the internet with no networking setup.

Can I run more than one scoreboard?

Yes — create as many independent boards (courts) as you need from one app, each with its own output and scorer.

Can I use my own images and logos?

Yes. Upload images right in the app (organised by show/event), or drive different images per row from a spreadsheet.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows and Mac.
